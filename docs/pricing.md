<h2>Questions about the editions</h2>
<div class="icl-faq">
<details><summary>Does a licence change what Studio does?</summary><p>No. A licence never limits ports, setups, capture or files, and nothing in Studio is switched on or off by it today. Studio reads the licence file, shows the edition and says what it covers. Gating of features is Planned.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can I use the Community edition at work?</summary><p>Yes. The GNU General Public License lets anyone use, change and share the code. <a href="../licensing/">Licensing</a> has the detail. Take your own advice for your case.</p></details>
<details><summary>What does Studio do when a licence ends?</summary><p>Studio becomes the Community edition and says so once. Your ports, setups and files are not lost or locked.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can I try Pro first?</summary><p>Ask us. The Community edition has the whole application, so you can try the software itself today. <a href="../support/#talk-to-us">Talk to us</a>.</p></details>
<details><summary>How do I buy?</summary><p>Write to us with the edition you want and how many people and computers will use it. <a href="../support/#talk-to-us">Talk to us</a> has the address.</p></details>
</div>
