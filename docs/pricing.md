<h2>Questions about the editions</h2>
<div class="icl-faq">
<details><summary>Does a licence change what Studio does?</summary><p>No. A licence never limits ports, setups, capture or files, and nothing in Studio is switched on or off by it today. Studio reads the licence file, shows your edition and says what the licence covers.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can I use the Community edition at work?</summary><p>Yes. The GNU General Public License lets anyone use, change and share the code. <a href="../licensing/">Licensing</a> has the detail. Take your own advice for your case.</p></details>
<details><summary>What happens when a licence ends?</summary><p>Studio becomes the Community edition and tells you once. Your ports, setups and files are not lost or locked.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can I try Pro first?</summary><p>Ask us. Community is the whole application, so you can try the software itself today. <a href="../support/#talk-to-us">Talk to us</a>.</p></details>
<details><summary>How do I buy?</summary><p>Tell us which edition you want and how many people and computers will use it. <a href="../support/#talk-to-us">Talk to us</a> has the form and the address.</p></details>
</div>
