**When a port does not work, Studio says why and how to fix it.** **Setup check** reads the facts the platform will give. On Linux it reads the owner group and mode of each serial device and says whether your account is missing the group (with `sudo usermod -aG dialout <you>` and a button that runs just that command), the mode forbids it, or something else refused it. A *Confinement* row explains Flatpak, Snap, AppArmor and SELinux, and a *Firewall* row shows the `ufw` or `firewalld` command that opens a port. On macOS it explains the Application Firewall, Local Network access and the App Sandbox. **Why is nothing arriving?** runs five checks against the live endpoint state and says "Not checked yet" rather than guessing. The **support bundle** (**File, Support bundle**) is a ZIP of the setup, event log, environment and counters to attach to a bug report; it never holds the bytes your ports carried. `vss_cli permissions check` prints the same facts.
