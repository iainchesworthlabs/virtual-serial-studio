**Simple mode is a guided list of jobs.** Studio opens on "What do you want to do?" with the jobs in plain words: give a program a serial port, connect two programs, use or share a real device, try a device from this window, let other computers use a port, put several programs on one line, send several into one, and let programs take turns. Each job is one form with a picture of what it builds. Each setup has its own page that says which port to open in which program, shows whether a program has opened it, warns when its baud rate differs from the device's, and lets you type to the device. **Remove setup** closes a pair, a share or an attached device with all of its ports. Up to 32 virtual ports can be made at once, bundled and recent setups are under **Setups**, and the window warns before it takes a port away from a program that has it open. On Windows a form offers only free COM numbers.
