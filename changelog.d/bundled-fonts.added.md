**Hebrew, Yiddish and Arabic text and byte columns look the same on every system.** Studio now carries three open-licence fonts: Noto Sans Hebrew and Noto Sans Arabic, for letters the system font cannot draw, and IBM Plex Mono for the hex, byte and monitor views, so the columns line up on Windows, macOS and Linux. No Chinese or Japanese font is included; the Linux packages recommend `fonts-noto-cjk` (Debian) or suggest `google-noto-sans-cjk-fonts` (RPM).
