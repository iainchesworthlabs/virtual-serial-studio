**The Monitor, Log and Topology JSON panes can be moved.** Each now has a title bar with a menu and a close button. Drag the title bar and guides appear: a centre square puts the pane in the same tabs as the pane under the cursor, and the four squares around it put it left, right, above or below that pane. Squares at the edges of the window dock the pane at that edge, and a shaded area shows where it will land. Let go outside the window and the pane floats. To use the keyboard instead, open the pane menu: **Float**, **Move to the window edge**, and **Beside** each other pane (tab with it, or left, right, above or below it). Closed panes come back from **View, Panes**, which shows a check mark for each open pane. **Next pane** and **Previous pane** (Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab) move between them. The arrangement is kept between runs; a saved arrangement that cannot be used is dropped and the usual layout is shown, and **Reset layout** puts the panes back in tabs at the bottom. Closing the Monitor now stays closed until you open it again.
