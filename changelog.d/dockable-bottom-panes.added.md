**The panes can be moved: Ports, Inspector, Send, Monitor, Log, Topology JSON and the Links list.** Each pane has a title bar with a menu and a close button. Drag the title bar, or a tab, and guides appear: a centre square puts the pane in the same tabs as the pane under the cursor, and the four squares around it put it left, right, above or below that pane. Squares at the window edges dock the pane there. Let go outside the window and it floats. From the keyboard, use the pane menu: **Float**, **Move to the window edge**, and **Beside** each other pane. Closed panes come back from **View, Panes**. **Next pane** and **Previous pane** (Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab) move between them. The arrangement is kept between runs, and **Reset layout** restores the start layout.
