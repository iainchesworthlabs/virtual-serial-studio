**Open in monitor now shows the port, and decoding and the monitor port can be found in the Advanced view.** **Open in monitor** (on a port, and on a monitor link) sets the Monitor's port filter to that port and says so in the status bar. With a decoder plugin switched on, a port's menu (and a monitor link's) offers **Decode as...**, which asks which decoder to use and starts it on that port; what it finds is shown in Plugins. The palette tiles for a fixture type such as **Recorded device** are disabled until the engine can play a recording as a device. **A monitor takes the settings of the port it watches.** In the Add monitor sheet, an existing port chosen as the watched end supplies its baud rate and framing. **Double-click opens Settings on the canvas.** In Advanced, double-clicking a port, network port, bus member, monitor or plugin item opens its Settings, and double-clicking a link opens the link's settings. A monitor link has no settings, so double-clicking it opens the port it watches in the Monitor. **A monitor can copy one direction.** Settings for a monitor link, and the Inspector of the port at its copy end, offer **Direction**: **Both directions** (the default), **Transmit only** (what the program on the port writes) or **Receive only** (what is written into the port). It is saved in the setup file.
