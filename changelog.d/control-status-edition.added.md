**The control gateway's status says which edition is in force.** The `status` reply now has a `licence` part with the edition (`community`, `pro` or `enterprise`) and the state of the licence, and `vss_cli control status` prints them as an `Edition:` line. Whose the licence is, its number and its dates are not given. `vss_gate` reads the licence folders as it starts and once a minute after that.
