**`vss_cli`, a command line that runs without a window.** It lists serial devices and com0com pairs (`devices`), creates virtual ports (`virtual`, `vport`), shares a device across ports (`share`), joins publishers (`join`), builds a bus (`bus`), bridges transports (`bridge`), makes a virtual port with a redirect and a monitor and no hardware (`loopback`), runs a saved topology (`run`), records and plays back traffic (`capture`, `play`), answers from a script (`respond`), measures throughput (`bench`) and checks the setup (`permissions check`, `selftest`). The window's **Export as command** turns the current setup into a `vss_cli` command. `vss_cli virtual` keeps its port open until Ctrl-C, or for `--seconds=N`.
