**An `rfc2217://host:port` port now sets the speed, DTR, RTS and break on the server.** Studio's RFC 2217 client sends the speed and framing in the address (`?baud=115200&data=8&parity=none&stop=1`) once the server agrees to the COM-PORT option. A change of DTR or RTS reaches the server, and the server's CTS, DSR, DCD and RI are shown as the port's inputs. A break at the port is sent to the server, and the log says its length on the wire is only as accurate as the network. A break the server reports is a break in the stream, one character time long. A server without RFC 2217 is used as a plain byte stream, and the log says so.
