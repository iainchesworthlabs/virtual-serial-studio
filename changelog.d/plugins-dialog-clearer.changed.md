**The Plugins window is clearer.** A row in the list shows a state beside the switch only when the state is neither On nor Off, so a row with a state is one to read. **Add a plugin...** is a button under the list, and **Import a key...**, **Open the plugin folder** and **Check again** are in a **More options** menu beside it. In the detail, what the plugin adds, who made it and what it may do come first. The fingerprint, licence, timing, isolation, who it is installed for and its folder are together at the end, under **Technical details**. The next step for a plugin that needs one, such as **Review and decide** or **Start again**, is a normal button, not a link. A plugin's timing is explained under its value, not only in a tooltip, the Virtual Serial Studio badge sits next to the signature sentence, and the detail no longer repeats a Plugin mark beside the name.
