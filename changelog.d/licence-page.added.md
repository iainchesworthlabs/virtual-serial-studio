**Help, Licence shows which edition Studio is and what a licence includes.** The edition chip at the right of the status bar opens the same page. It reads Community, Pro or Enterprise, and counts the days left when a licence is about to end. The page lists who the licence was issued to, its number, term and seats, and whether it is valid, ending soon, in its grace period, ended, not started or not valid. For official plugins, the Microsoft-signed driver and support it says whether each is available and why not. **Import licence...** takes a `.vsslic` file from a file dialog or dropped on the page, and copies it to your licence folder only when its signature is good; a file that is not valid is refused with the reason. **Remove licence** asks first, then takes the copy out of your licence folder; the file you imported from is untouched. A banner at the top of the window says when a licence is about to end, has ended, or the date on the computer looks wrong; **Dismiss** and **Remind me tomorrow** hide it for a while. Studio never contacts a server, so **How to renew** only copies the address and the licence number.
