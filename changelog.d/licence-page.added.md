**Help, Licence shows which edition Studio is and what a licence includes.** The edition chip at the right of the status bar opens the same page. It reads Community, Pro or Enterprise, and counts the days left when a licence is about to end. The page lists who the licence was issued to, its number, term and seats, and whether it is valid, ending soon, in its grace period, ended, not started yet or not valid. For official plugins, the Microsoft-signed driver and support it says whether each is available and, when it is not, why. **Import licence...** takes a `.vsslic` file from a file dialog or dropped on the page, and copies it to your licence folder only when its signature is good. A file that is not valid is refused with the reason and nothing changes. **Remove licence** asks first, then takes the copy out of your licence folder; the file you imported from is untouched. A banner under the other banners at the top of the window says when a licence is about to end, has ended, or the date on the computer looks wrong. **Dismiss** hides the notice of a licence that is ending until 7 days before the end and then until the last day, and **Remind me tomorrow** brings the notice for the grace period back the next day. Studio checks a licence on this computer and never contacts a server, so **How to renew** only copies the address to write to and the licence number.
