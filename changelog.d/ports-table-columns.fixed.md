**The Advanced Ports table columns can be resized.** Drag a header divider to change a column's width, or double-click it to fit the column to its contents; the last column takes the spare width, so there is no gap on the right, and the table scrolls sideways if the columns are wider than the dock. The State chip ("Waiting for program") is no longer cut off at the default width. With the cursor in the table, Left and Right choose a column, Ctrl+Alt+Left and Ctrl+Alt+Right narrow and widen it, and Ctrl+Alt+0 fits it. The widths are kept for the next start.
