**The Advanced Ports table columns can be resized.** Drag a header divider to change a column's width, or double-click it to fit the column to its contents. The State chip ("Waiting for program") is no longer cut off at the default width. With the cursor in the table, Left and Right choose a column, Ctrl+Alt+Left and Ctrl+Alt+Right narrow and widen it, and Ctrl+Alt+0 fits it.
