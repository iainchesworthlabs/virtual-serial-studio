**A network port, or a serial device, can be paused and resumed, and the setup remembers the pause.** Choose **Pause** in the port's menu on the canvas or in the Inspector, to close it for a while: a network client lets go of the other computer and does not try again, a server lets go of its address and drops its clients, and a serial device is closed. The port keeps its name and its links, and what is sent to it while paused is lost. **Resume** opens it again. A paused port shows an accent tile and a **Paused** chip, and reads **Paused** in the ports list and the Inspector. Unlike a stopped port, it is saved in the setup file as `"paused": true`.
