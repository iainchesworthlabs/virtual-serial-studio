**Adding or closing a program in a group sending into one receiver, or on a shared line (bus), no longer interrupts the link or loses bytes for the others.** In Simple mode, **Add a program**, the close button and **Change…** now add the port to the setup's link or take it off in place, as they already do for a shared device: the link keeps its identity, options and counters, and the other ports keep their bytes on the way. Changing the receiver of a group, turning a bus into some other job, or swapping most of a bus of 64 members still makes the link again, and so does a pair, a lone port with an echo or a device other computers use. If a step fails, the setup is as it was.
