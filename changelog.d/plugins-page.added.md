**Tools, Plugins... shows the plugins installed on this computer.** Each plugin has a row with its trust line (Official, Signed by someone, Unsigned, or signed by an author you have not approved), its version, its state and a switch. The detail shows what it adds, who made it, its signature and fingerprint, how far this computer can keep it apart from your files, what it may do and its recent messages. **Review and decide** asks whether to run it, for that version only, and lists every permission it asks for in plain sentences; optional permissions can be unticked. **Import a key...** approves an author's public key after you compare its fingerprint with the one the author published, and **Run without isolation...** is a separate question. The Advanced toolbox has a **Plugins** group with a tile for each fixture type of a plugin that is switched on; a port that a plugin provides is marked **Plugin not available** when the plugin cannot be used; notices at the top of the window say that a plugin was found, changed, stopped or withdrawn; and a status bar chip says **Reading data** or **Recording**. **Add a plugin...** shows what it found before anything is copied.
