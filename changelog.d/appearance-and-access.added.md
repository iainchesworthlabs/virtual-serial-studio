**A clear, accessible look in a light and a dark theme.** The theme follows the system's light or dark setting, or you can pick one; every pair of colours meets WCAG AA contrast. Every control has an accessible name and role, can be reached from the keyboard, shows a focus ring and is at least 24 by 24 pixels, and every dialog works at 200 percent text. `Ctrl+K` opens a command palette and **Keyboard shortcuts** lists them.
