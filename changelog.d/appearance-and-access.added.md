**A clear, accessible look in a light and a dark theme.** The window is a tinted ground with fill buttons (a blue primary, a soft grey secondary), outlined fields with a blue edge when focused, tinted capsules for state chips and RX, TX and DROP tags, a segmented Simple and Advanced switch and pill tabs. The theme follows the system's light or dark setting, or you can pick one; every pair of colours meets WCAG AA contrast. Icons are drawn at any size and display scale in the colour the theme has at that moment, and text stays sharp at 125 and 150 percent. Every control has an accessible name and role, can be reached from the keyboard, shows a focus ring and is at least 24 by 24 pixels, and every dialog works at 200 percent text. `Ctrl+K` opens a command palette over every action and **Keyboard shortcuts** lists them. The window stays responsive while ports are busy: bursts of traffic are coalesced rather than repainting for each batch.
