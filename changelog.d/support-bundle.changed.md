**The diagnostic bundle is now the support bundle, and Help, Support says how to get help.** **File, Support bundle** (it was "Diagnostic bundle") makes `vss-support-<date>.zip`. It never holds the bytes your ports sent and received: the "Traffic contents" choice and `traffic.txt` are gone. The list has a row for each part: Version and environment, Driver state, Setup, Event log, Port counters, Serial ports on this computer, and Licence when one is installed. Two amber rows, off until you turn them on, add the licensee name and this computer's name. Always left out: passwords and tokens, the path of your home folder, your computer and user names, device serial numbers and the licence file. The right side previews each file as it will be saved, and a file over 256 kB shows its first 256 kB. **Help, Support...** has four cards: where to get help, what to write before you ask, the support bundle, and the version, edition, licence number and operating system to copy. It only copies to the clipboard and sends nothing.
