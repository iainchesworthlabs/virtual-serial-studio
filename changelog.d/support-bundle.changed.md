**The diagnostic bundle is now the support bundle, and Help, Support says how to get help.** **File, Support bundle** (it was "Diagnostic bundle") makes a file named `vss-support-<date>.zip`. It never holds the bytes your ports sent and received: the "Traffic contents" choice and `traffic.txt` are gone. The list now has a row for each part: Version and environment (with the edition), Driver state, Setup, Event log, Port counters, Serial ports on this computer (name, description, vendor and product ids, and a "not known" driver), and Licence when one is installed. Two amber rows, off until you turn them on, add the licensee name and this computer's name to `licence.txt`. A box says what is always left out: passwords and tokens, the path of your home folder, your computer and user names, device serial numbers and the licence file. The right side previews each file, with its size, exactly as it will be saved, and a row you untick leaves the list at once. A file over 256 kB shows its first 256 kB and says so, and the saved file is whole. The zip starts with `manifest.json`, which lists the parts you chose. After saving, the toast offers **Show in folder**. A save that fails offers **Choose another place**. **Help, Support...** has four cards: where to get help for your edition, what to write before you ask, the support bundle, and the version, edition, licence number and operating system to copy. It only copies addresses to the clipboard: it opens no web page and no mail program and sends nothing. About now also says which edition this is, and its footer link and the Troubleshoot footer say "Create support bundle".
