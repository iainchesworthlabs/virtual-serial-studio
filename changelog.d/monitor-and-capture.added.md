**Watch traffic, record it with exact times, and play it back.** The Monitor shows the last 10,000 transfers as ASCII and hex with real DROP rows, pause, follow and clear, and port, direction and search filters. `vss_cli capture <topology.json | --uri=...> --out=FILE [--format=cap|csv|jsonl]` writes every transfer with its time in milliseconds from the start of the capture on one clock for every port, the port it belongs to, a direction (`R` is bytes the port received, `W` is bytes written to it) and all of its bytes; the monitor's **Export capture** button writes the visible rows the same way. `vss_cli play FILE --uri=URI` writes the records of one direction into a port at the times they were recorded (`--speed`, `--loop`), so a recorded master or device can drive a bus without the program that made the recording.
