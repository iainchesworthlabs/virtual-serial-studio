**Links on the canvas are drawn as smooth curves, or at right angles if you prefer.** A link now runs level out of its port, bends in one smooth S and runs level into the next port, the same shape the wire has while you drag it, instead of snapping to an angled line when you let go. Under **Settings → Appearance → Link lines** you can switch to **Right angles**: level out of the port, a turn in the middle of the gap and a turn into the target, with rounded corners. Curved is the default and the choice applies at once. A link that has to go round another box keeps clear of it and takes the way that crosses the fewest other links, a split or a join fans out from a junction in the middle of the gap, and a bus bar turns into its end members in round corners. Two links on the same pair of ports bend apart so you can see both, and the boxes in a column are ordered to leave as few crossings as possible.
