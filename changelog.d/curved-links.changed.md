**Links on the canvas are drawn as smooth curves, or at right angles if you prefer.** A link now runs level out of its port, bends in one smooth S and runs level into the next port, the same shape the wire has while you drag it. Under **Settings → Appearance → Link lines** you can switch to **Right angles**. Curved is the default and the choice applies at once. A link that has to go round another box keeps clear of it.
