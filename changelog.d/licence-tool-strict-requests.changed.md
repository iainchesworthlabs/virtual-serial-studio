**`vss_license` refuses a request that does not say what it seems to, and `verify` counts the built-in revocation list.** `issue` and `revocations` refuse a request with an unknown field and name it, so a misspelt `machines` no longer gives a licence with no computer binding. `issue` also refuses a request with no `features`, a non-empty `critical`, a `licence_id` or `replaces` not of the form `VSS-<year>-<six digits>`, an `issued_at` more than 48 hours ahead of the clock or after `expires_at`, and a trial with `grace_days` not 0, `seats` not 1 or named computers. `verify` uses the built-in revocation list, as Studio does, unless a `--revocations` file has a higher sequence number, and prints which it used.
