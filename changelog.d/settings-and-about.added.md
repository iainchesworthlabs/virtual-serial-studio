**Settings, About and licences.** **Settings** (Ctrl+,) holds Advanced mode, theme, density, refresh interval, default baud rate and whether to ask before discarding an unsaved setup; each applies at once. **About** shows the licence notice, the version and build, an Environment tab (commit, compiler, Qt and Boost versions, operating system) to copy into a bug report, and Licences. Virtual Serial Studio is released under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later; Boost.VirtualSerial, Boost and the tools derived from them keep their own licences (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`). **Settings, Performance, Profiling**, `--profile` or `VSS_PROFILE=1` turns on Tracy profiling, which listens on 127.0.0.1 unless `--profile-remote` is given (`docs/profiling.md`).
