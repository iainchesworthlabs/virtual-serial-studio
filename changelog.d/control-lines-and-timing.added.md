**RS-232 control lines and link timing.** DTR, RTS and BREAK are driven per port, CTS, DSR, DCD and RI are reported, and a null-modem link crosses DTR to DSR and RTS to CTS. For a mock fixture, **Open line matrix and faults** in the Inspector lets you choose which of DTR and RTS drives which of DSR, CTS, DCD and RI on the linked port, or pick a preset; it applies at once and is not saved. Any link, a bus included, can hold what it carries for a set time with `delay_ms`, varied by up to `jitter_ms` either side.
