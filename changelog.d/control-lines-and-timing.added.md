**RS-232 control lines and link timing.** DTR, RTS and BREAK are driven per port, CTS, DSR, DCD and RI are reported, and a null-modem link crosses DTR to DSR and RTS to CTS so handshaking code can be exercised without hardware. For a mock fixture, **Open line matrix and faults** in the Inspector lets you choose which of DTR and RTS drives which of DSR, CTS, DCD and RI on the linked port, or pick a preset, and the change applies at once (the default is the null-modem cable; the wiring lasts while Studio runs and is not saved). Any link, a shared bus included, can hold what it carries for a set time with `delay_ms`, varied by up to `jitter_ms` either side, so you can rehearse a reply that arrives at 190 ms or 210 ms, or the extra hop of a network bridge. Bytes of one route keep their order.
