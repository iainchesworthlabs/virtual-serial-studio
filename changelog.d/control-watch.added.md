**Watching the ports from the command line.** `vss_cli control watch` prints one line for each event on the ports through the gateway: the batch number, the time, the kind, the port, the direction, the size and the bytes in hex. `--data` gives the bytes and needs the scope `monitor.data`. `--ports`, `--interval`, `--seconds` and `--count` choose what to hear about and when to stop, and a line that starts with `#` says how many events were lost because the reader was too slow. `vss_cli control sessions` lists who is connected and `vss_cli control kick <number>` closes one session.
