**Watching the ports from the command line.** `vss_cli control watch` prints what happens to the ports through the gateway, one line for each event: the batch number, the time in nanoseconds on the steady clock, the kind, the port, the direction, the size and the bytes in hex. With `--data` the bytes are given, which needs the scope `monitor.data`; without it a line has none. `--ports`, `--interval`, `--seconds` and `--count` choose what to hear about and when to stop, and a line that starts with `#` says how many events were lost because the reader was too slow. `vss_cli control sessions` lists who is connected to the gateway and `vss_cli control kick <number>` closes one session; both go through the administration endpoint.
