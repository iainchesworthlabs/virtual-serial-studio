**Changing a link's Settings no longer makes the link again.** On the Advanced canvas, **Settings...** on a link (delay, jitter, what happens when two write at once, emulate modem lines) now changes the link where it is, keeping its identity, ports, owner and counters, and Undo and Redo change the settings back the same way. Bytes the link still holds for its delay are discarded when the delay changes; other changes lose none. Turning modem lines on gives the ports on the link the lines the other end drives at once, and turning them off drops the lines the link drove. A shared bus takes delay, jitter and its half-duplex line. A monitor has no settings.
