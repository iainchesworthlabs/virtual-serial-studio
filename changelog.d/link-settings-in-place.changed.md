**Changing a link's Settings no longer makes the link again.** On the Advanced canvas, **Settings...** on a link (delay, jitter, what happens when two write at once, emulate modem lines) now changes the link where it is: it keeps its identity, its ports, its owner and its counters, and Undo and Redo change the settings back in the same way. Bytes the link still holds for its delay are discarded when the delay changes, as when a port is taken off a delayed link; other changes lose none. Choosing Mix them clears the owner. Turning modem lines on gives the ports on the link the lines the other end drives at once, and turning them off drops the lines the link drove unless another link drives them. A shared bus takes delay, jitter and its half-duplex line; changing the half-duplex line discards what is on the wire at that moment and starts the bus's collision count again. A monitor has no settings.
