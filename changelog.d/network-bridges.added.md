**Bridge a serial port to the network, as a client or a server.** `tcp://`, `udp://` and `rfc2217://` endpoints are first-class ports. `tcp-listen://host:port` accepts any number of TCP clients and sends them everything written to it, `udp-listen://host:port` reads datagrams from anyone and replies to senders it has heard from lately, and `rfc2217-listen://host:port` makes Studio an RFC 2217 server that a client such as pySerial's `rfc2217://` can open as a serial port, with DTR and RTS reaching the linked port and the modem lines coming back. (The baud rate and framing a client asks for are accepted but not applied to a real device.) The **Put a device on the network** dialog and `vss_cli bridge` take these addresses. `vss_cli run --reconnect` opens a dropped `tcp://` or `rfc2217://` client again, after a delay that doubles from one second to thirty.
