**The canvas repaints faster while you resize the window.** The dot grid behind the topology was drawn as round dots, which cost about 50 ms for every repaint of a large canvas and made dragging a window corner stutter. Studio also writes a line to `stalls.log` in its local data folder when the window stops answering for two seconds, naming what it was doing (this stays on your computer), and the performance trace is now available in Release builds too and remembered between starts (Settings, Performance, Profiling).
