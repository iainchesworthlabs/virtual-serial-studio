**The Log has the Monitor's layout, density and filters.** Rows and column headings are as tall as the Monitor's in Comfortable and Compact, change when you change View, Density, and grow with larger text, and Time, Port and Event are as wide as their headings and contents need. The "Event log" band and the title strip above the table are gone: a port picker, Errors, Started, Stopped and Other check boxes, a search box and Clear share the "Log" title row, and drop to a row under it when the pane is too narrow. Error and bus fault rows are in the error colour with the Event word in semi-bold. New events keep arriving while a filter is on, and the view only follows the newest row when it was already at the end.
