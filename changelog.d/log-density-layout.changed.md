**The Log has the Monitor's layout, density and filters, and the Topology JSON tab has a slimmer toolbar.** Rows and column headings in the Log are as tall as the Monitor's in Comfortable and Compact, change when you change View, Density, and grow with larger text, and Time, Port and Event are as wide as their headings and contents need. The "Event log" band is gone: a port picker, an Events menu for Errors, Started, Stopped and Other, a search box and Clear share the Log's title row. In a narrow pane the search box moves to a second row, so no control is squeezed or cut off. Error and bus fault rows are in the error colour with the Event word in semi-bold. New events keep arriving while a filter is on, and the view only follows the newest row when it was already at the end. The Topology JSON tab's toolbar is one slim row, with its hint as a tooltip.
