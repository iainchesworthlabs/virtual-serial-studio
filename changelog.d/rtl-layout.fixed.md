**Hebrew, Yiddish and Arabic read correctly.** A port name or path such as `loopback:logger` or `COM3` inside a translated sentence is now kept in its own order, so a sentence no longer jumbles its names. The monitor, log, byte boxes, times and addresses stay left to right. The copy button on a port row no longer covers the path, and the arrows of redirect, split, join, share, loopback and run point the way the text reads.
