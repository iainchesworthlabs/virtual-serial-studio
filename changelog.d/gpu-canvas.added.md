**The canvas can be drawn on the graphics card.** Settings, Performance, Rendering chooses Automatic, Hardware (GPU) or Software (CPU), and applies at the next start. The grid, the nodes and the links are drawn by the GPU, and Studio goes back to the processor on its own, and says so, when there is no usable graphics device. `--render=auto|hardware|software` and `VSS_RENDER` choose for one run, and About, Environment shows which one is in use. The performance trace gains render zones and a GPU time for the canvas. Qt Canvas Painter (Qt 6.11 or newer) is now needed to build the GUI.
