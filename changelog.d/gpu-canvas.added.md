**The canvas can be drawn on the graphics card.** **Settings, Performance, Rendering** chooses Automatic, Hardware (GPU) or Software (CPU), and applies at the next start. Studio goes back to the processor, and says so, when there is no usable graphics device. `--render=auto|hardware|software` and `VSS_RENDER` choose for one run. Building the GUI now needs Qt Canvas Painter (Qt 6.11 or newer).
