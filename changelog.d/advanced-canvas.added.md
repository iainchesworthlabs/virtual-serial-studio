**Advanced mode is a canvas editor.** A toolbox beside the topology holds ports (Port, Fixture, Loopback, Device, Network) and bundles (Pair, Split, Join, Bus, Switch, Redirect, Monitor, Share, Bench). Drag a tile onto the canvas, or press Enter on it, and choose in the Add sheet whether each end is a new port, a new fixture, a loopback or a port already there, so a pair of two fixtures is one step. Drag from the handle on a port's edge to another port to link them two-way, one way or as a monitor; drop on empty canvas to add a new port there. Right-click a port for its serial settings (baud rate, data bits, parity, stop bits, flow control and timeouts), Duplicate and Delete; right-click a link for its delay, jitter and policy. Select several items with Ctrl+click, a rubber band or Ctrl+A. Nodes can be dragged or moved with the arrow keys and links route around them, Fit and Tidy layout keep everything in view, **F2** renames a port, and Undo and Redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z) cover connecting and removing links, changing an owner and renaming. The topology JSON can be edited live with errors reported on the line at fault, and an event log, **Export as command** and owner arbitration for shared links are in the same window.
