**Advanced mode is a canvas editor.** A toolbox beside the topology holds ports (Port, Fixture, Loopback, Device, Network) and bundles (Pair, Split, Join, Bus, Switch, Redirect, Monitor, Share, Bench). Drag a tile onto the canvas, or press Enter on it, and choose in the Add sheet whether each end is a new port, a new fixture, a loopback or a port already there. Drag from the handle on a port's edge to another port to link them two-way, one way or as a monitor. Right-click a port for its serial settings; right-click a link for its delay, jitter and policy. Nodes can be dragged or moved with the arrow keys, Fit and Tidy layout keep everything in view, **F2** renames a port, and Undo and Redo are supported. The topology JSON can be edited live with errors reported on the line at fault. An event log and **Export as command** are in the same window.
