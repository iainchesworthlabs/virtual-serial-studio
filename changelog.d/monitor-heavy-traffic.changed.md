**The Monitor costs the window much less under heavy traffic.** New rows are put in the table at most 30 times a second instead of once for every batch of events, the rows of a batch share one reading of the clock and one block of memory for their bytes, and a long preview is no longer laid out in full to show the part that fits its cell. With four ports echoing as fast as they can, the window handles about three times as many events in the same time and is busy for less of it. The Monitor shows the same rows and text as before. The rows of one batch now carry the same time; before they could differ by a millisecond or two.
