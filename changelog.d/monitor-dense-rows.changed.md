**The Monitor shows more rows.** Rows are as tall as their text needs, 24 px at the least (they were 28). Columns are wide enough that "Direction" is no longer cut off. The Density setting now reaches the Monitor. Columns can be resized by dragging a divider or with `Ctrl+Alt+Left`, `Ctrl+Alt+Right` and `Ctrl+Alt+0` (fit), and the widths are kept.
