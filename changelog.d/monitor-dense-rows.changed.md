**The Monitor shows more rows.** Rows are as tall as their text needs, 24 px at the least (they were 28), and the header is as tall as its text needs. The columns are as wide as their headings and contents in every language, so "Direction" is no longer cut off. The Density setting now reaches the Monitor: Compact has less space at the sides of each cell, and its rows are shorter than Comfortable once the text is larger than normal. Columns can be resized by dragging a divider or with `Ctrl+Alt+Left`, `Ctrl+Alt+Right` and `Ctrl+Alt+0` (fit), and the widths are kept.
