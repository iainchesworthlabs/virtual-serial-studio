**The keyboard focus ring goes around the whole control, clear of its label, and stays while you use the keyboard.** It was drawn around a button's text, so on **Cancel** and other buttons it cut across the last letter. It now goes around the button, check box, radio button, switch, tab, slider or tool button itself. Sliders, tool buttons, tabs, text areas and links in labels now have a mark too. The ring no longer disappears after Alt+Tab or after a menu or list closes, and a dialog opened with Enter shows it on its default button. A mouse click hides the ring until you press a key again.
