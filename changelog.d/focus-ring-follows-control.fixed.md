**The keyboard focus ring goes around the whole control and stays while you use the keyboard.** It was drawn around a button's text, so on **Cancel** and other buttons it cut across the last letter. It now goes around the button, check box, switch, tab, slider or tool button itself, just inside its edge, and sliders, tool buttons and tabs now have one. It also no longer disappears after Alt+Tab, after a menu or list closes, or when a dialog opens from the keyboard: a dialog opened with Enter shows it on its default button. A mouse click hides the ring until you press a key again.
