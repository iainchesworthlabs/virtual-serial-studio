**The keyboard focus ring goes around the whole control, clear of its label, and stays while you use the keyboard.** It was drawn around a button's text, so on **Cancel** and other buttons it cut across the last letter. It now goes around the button, check box, radio button, switch, tab, slider or tool button itself, just inside its edge, with a gap so it never touches the label or the box. Sliders, tool buttons, tabs, text areas and links in labels that you reach with Tab now have a mark too. The ring also no longer disappears after Alt+Tab, after a menu or list closes, or when a dialog opens from the keyboard: a dialog opened with Enter shows it on its default button. A mouse click hides the ring until you press a key again. Check boxes, radio buttons, switches and sliders are a few pixels larger to make room for it.
