**The Monitor stays where you put it when Follow is off.** Scrolling up with the wheel or the scroll bar, pressing `Page Up` or `Up`, going to the first row with `Home`, or selecting a row that is not the newest now unticks **Follow** (in the Simple toolbar and the Advanced controls alike), and the next rows no longer pull the table back to the bottom. Scrolling back to the end, `End` or `Ctrl+End`, or ticking **Follow** turns it on again and goes to the newest row. Before, ticked or not, a busy port kept the table moving: once the history was full, every batch removed old rows from the top and the rows on screen shifted up by that many, so the row you had selected moved out of view. Now the same rows stay in the same place while new rows arrive. If the rows you are looking at are themselves the oldest ones and leave the history, the table stays at the top of what is left and the selection is cleared, and it does not jump to the bottom. **Pause**, **Resume** and **Clear** do not change Follow.
