**The Monitor stays where you put it when Follow is off.** Scrolling up, pressing `Page Up` or `Up`, going to the first row with `Home`, or selecting a row that is not the newest now unticks **Follow**, and new rows no longer pull the table back to the bottom. Scrolling back to the end, `End` or `Ctrl+End`, or ticking **Follow** turns it on again and goes to the newest row. Before, a busy port moved the row you had selected out of view. If the rows you are looking at leave the history, the selection is cleared. **Pause**, **Resume** and **Clear** do not change Follow.
