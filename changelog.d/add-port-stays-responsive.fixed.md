**The window no longer freezes while Windows installs new ports.** Choosing **Create** or **Apply** in Simple view, or **Create port** and **Create pair** in Advanced view, used to hold the whole window for 15 to 20 seconds while Windows installed the virtual ports in Device Manager, with nothing to show the click had registered. The ports are now made in the background. The button changes to **Creating** or **Applying**, a progress bar and a sentence say that Studio is setting up and why it takes a while, the rest of the form is disabled so a second click starts nothing, and the window stays responsive. If it fails, the form comes back with the same message as before and nothing is changed. The dialog stays open until the ports are made.
