**A listening port has limits on what a client can make it do.** It accepts up to 32 clients at once and closes any more as soon as they connect, with a note in the log. When the computer cannot accept a connection, for example when the system's file limit is used up, the port waits 100 ms and then longer, up to 5 seconds, before it tries again, and says so in the log, instead of using a whole processor. An RFC 2217 client that does not read its replies is disconnected when they reach 1 MiB.
