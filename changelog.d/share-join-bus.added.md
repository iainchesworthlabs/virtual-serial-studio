**Share one port with many programs, merge many into one, or put them all on one line.** A real port can be split across any number of virtual ports, several publishers can be joined into one target, and a one-way redirect or a monitor link lets you watch traffic without disturbing it. A **bus** link puts several ports on one multi-drop line: every port hears every other and never itself, any kind of port can be a member, and a join from the members gives a listen-only tap. A bus carries no modem lines, does not report two stations sending at once and does not hold the line for a turn-around time. Links that would form a loop are refused. Links share a device either interleaved or with a single owner, and the owner can be changed while it runs.
