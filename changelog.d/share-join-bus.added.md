**Share one port with many programs, merge many into one, or put them all on one line.** A real port can be split across any number of virtual ports, several publishers can be joined into one target, and a one-way redirect or a monitor link lets you watch traffic. A **bus** link puts several ports on one multi-drop line: every port hears every other and never itself, and any kind of port can be a member. A bus carries no modem lines and, unless you make it a half-duplex line, does not report two stations sending at once. Links that would form a loop are refused.
