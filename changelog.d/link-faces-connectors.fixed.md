**A link joins the sides of two items that face each other.** A link from an item to another item on its left used to leave the first item by its right-hand connector and arrive at the second by its left-hand connector, so it wrapped round both items. It now leaves by the side towards the other item and arrives by the side towards the first, with curves and with right angles, and it follows when you move an item across the other. When you draw a link by dragging from a connector on an item's edge, the link keeps to that connector for as long as Studio is open.
