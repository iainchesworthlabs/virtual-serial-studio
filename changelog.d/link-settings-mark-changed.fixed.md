**Changing a link's settings or a port's serial settings now marks the setup as changed.** The title did not show the unsaved mark after you changed a link's delay, jitter, policy or modem lines in **Link settings**, or a port's baud rate, data bits, parity, stop bits, flow control or timeouts in **Settings**, so closing the window or opening another setup did not ask whether to save. Undo and Redo of these changes also mark the setup as changed.
