**Settings no longer sit under a doubled registry key.** On Windows Studio kept its settings in `HKCU\Software\Virtual Serial Studio\Virtual Serial Studio`. They are now in `HKCU\Software\iainchesworthlabs\Virtual Serial Studio`, and the saved running setup is in `%APPDATA%\iainchesworthlabs\Virtual Serial Studio`. On Linux and macOS the configuration files move in the same way. Settings made in earlier builds are not carried over.
