**Line rates go up to 12000000 baud.** The baud rate lists in the port settings, the Add port sheet, the Attach device form and the share wizard now offer 1000000, 2000000, 3000000, 4000000 and 12000000, and a rate up to 12000000 can be typed. A half-duplex bus line rate takes 50 to 12000000 bit/s. Whether a rate works depends on the system and device (a macOS pseudo terminal refuses 921600); Studio reports the refusal.
