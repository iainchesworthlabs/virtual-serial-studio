**Line rates go up to 12000000 baud.** The baud rate lists in the port settings, the Add port sheet, the Attach device form and the share wizard now offer 1000000, 2000000, 3000000, 4000000 and 12000000, and a rate up to 12000000 can be typed. The line rate of a half-duplex bus takes the same range, 50 to 12000000 bit/s. Whether a rate works still depends on the system and the device: a pseudo terminal on macOS refuses 921600, and some USB serial adapters stop below 12000000, so Studio reports the refusal when the rate is applied.
