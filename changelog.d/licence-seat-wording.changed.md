**The Licence page says what a seat is in the settled words.** Under the seat count of a Pro licence it now reads "A seat is two computers: the one you work at and one other that you control or monitor from it. The serial device can be on either." An Enterprise licence shows its seat count with "As agreed in your Enterprise licence.", because what such a licence covers is negotiated. A licence with no seat count reads "Not counted by seat" where it read "Site licence", on the page, in the support bundle's `licence.txt` and in `vss_license inspect`.
