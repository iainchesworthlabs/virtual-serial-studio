**New library calls `hub::add_source`, `hub::remove_source`, `hub::add_member` and `hub::remove_member`** add or take off one source of a one-way link (a join, a redirect or a monitor) and one endpoint of a bus, without touching the other routes: the link keeps its id, options, owner and counters. They refuse a link of the wrong kind (`unsupported`), an unknown link or endpoint (`not_found`), a duplicate (`already_exists`), a loop (`cycle_detected`), and taking off the last source or going below two bus members (`invalid_argument`).
