**Simple view: "Let other computers use my device" now waits for other computers.** The job used to open a connection out to the address instead of listening, so no other computer could ever connect. It now listens on the network port you choose (4001 unless you change it), on every address of this PC, and the speed, data bits, parity, stop bits and flow control chosen in the form reach the device. Changing a setup of this kind closes the old one first, because both may want the same device and port. If the new one cannot be made, Studio makes the old one again, and the message says whether it was put back or could not be.
