**Simple view: "Let other computers use my device" now waits for other computers.** The job used to open a connection out to the address instead of listening, so no other computer could connect. It now listens on the network port you choose (4001 unless you change it), on every address of this PC, and the speed, data bits, parity, stop bits and flow control chosen in the form reach the device. Changing such a setup closes the device and the network port and makes them again, so connected computers lose their connection, and the form's note says so. If the new one cannot be made, Studio restores the old one. Other bridges, such as an allow list, RFC 2217 or a client, are still changed in Advanced view.
