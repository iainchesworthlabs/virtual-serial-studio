**Adding or closing a program port of a shared device no longer interrupts the link or loses bytes for the other programs.** In the share wizard (**Add another program**), under **Topology → Add a program port**, when you remove a program port, and in Simple mode with **Add a program**, the close button and **Change…** on a shared or attached device or on programs taking turns, Studio now adds the port to the device's link or takes it off in place. The link keeps its identity, its options, its owner and its counters, and the other programs keep their ports and the bytes on their way. Before, the link was replaced, so a few bytes in flight at that moment could be lost. A setup that has no such edit in the engine, such as a pair, a bus, a group sending into one receiver or a device other computers use, is still made again when you change it. If a step fails, the setup is as it was.
