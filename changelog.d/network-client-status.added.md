**A network client shows where it connects and how that is going.** The client's pill on the Advanced canvas now shows the address and port it connects to, as a server's pill shows the address it waits on. The network icon turns while Studio is trying to connect, or shows a still arrow with reduced motion. A connected client has a green tile and a **Connected** chip, and one that could not connect shows a warning icon and a **Failed** chip. The ports list and the Inspector give the state (Connecting, Connected, Failed or Retrying) and a sentence that names the target and, for a failure, the reason, for example "Could not connect to tcp://192.168.1.50:4001: Connection refused. Trying again." The Inspector of a client also lists the remote address and port, when the connection came up, the last error, what was received and sent since it came up (bytes and reads or writes) and how many times it has reconnected. It also gives the last error with its code, when the next try is and the last ten events with their times, so a connection that keeps dropping can be followed. A state stays on the pill for at least a moment, so a connection that drops and returns does not make it flicker. The local address and port are not reported by the engine yet, and the Inspector says so.
