**A network client shows where it connects and how that is going.** The client's pill on the Advanced canvas shows its address and port. A connected client has a green tile and a **Connected** chip, and one that could not connect shows a warning icon and a **Failed** chip. The ports list and the Inspector give the state (Connecting, Connected, Failed or Retrying) and a sentence that names the target and, for a failure, the reason, for example "Could not connect to tcp://192.168.1.50:4001: Connection refused. Trying again." The Inspector of a client also lists the remote address and port, bytes sent and received, the reconnect count, the last error and when the next try is. The local address and port are not reported by the engine yet, and the Inspector says so.
