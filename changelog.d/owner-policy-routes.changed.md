**The owner policy now arbitrates the routes that meet at one endpoint.** On a join or a redirect that is the routes from the sources, so the owner is one of the sources, as before. On a two-way link or a split it is the routes from the targets back to the source, so `set_owner` accepts only a target there (`invalid_argument` for the source or any other endpoint), what the source sends reaches every target whoever owns the link, and the source never takes ownership. Before, the rule was checked on every route of a link: on a split the targets' replies were dropped once the source owned it, a target that sent first took the link and shut the source out, and only the source could be named the owner. `set_owner` on a bus returns `unsupported`, as before.
