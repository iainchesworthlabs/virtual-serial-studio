**The owner policy now arbitrates the routes that meet at one endpoint.** On a join or a redirect that is the routes from the sources, so the owner is one of the sources, as before. On a two-way link or a split it is the routes from the targets back to the source, so `set_owner` accepts only a target there (`invalid_argument` otherwise), what the source sends reaches every target whoever owns the link, and the source never takes ownership. `set_owner` on a bus returns `unsupported`, as before.
