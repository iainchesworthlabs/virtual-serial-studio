**The Monitor controls sit on the title row in Advanced mode.** The port filter, RX, TX and Dropped, payload search, ASCII and Hex, Pause, Clear, Follow, Export and Export capture now share the row with the "Monitor" title, so the empty bar under it is gone. When the pane is too narrow, or the text too large, for them all, they drop to a row of their own under the title instead of being cut off.
