**The window can be shown in French, Spanish, German, Hebrew, Yiddish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Arabic.** Studio takes the system's preferred languages in order, matches each by its language alone (any `en-*` is English, Chinese is Simplified unless the tag says Traditional) and loads the first catalogue it has. Hebrew, Yiddish and Arabic switch the layout to right to left. `VSS_LANGUAGE` names one to try, and the About window and diagnostic bundle show the language chosen. Each catalogue covers the roughly 1,130 distinct texts of the window. **They are machine translations that no speaker has reviewed**, which each catalogue and `docs/i18n.md` say; reports of wrong or awkward text are welcome.
