**Stand in for a device you do not have.** A mock fixture is a scriptable port. It can echo, or generate bytes (a text line, a fixed pattern, a counter or random bytes at a set rate), and **Send bytes**, **Start or Stop repeat** and **Open in monitor** are in every port's right-click menu (a repeat stays on the port it started on). In Advanced mode a fixture can be given faults: a parity error, framing error, overrun, break, hang-up or disconnect, reported as an error event and listed in the history. `vss_cli respond SCRIPT --uri=URI` makes a port answer from a JSON script: a rule says "when these bytes arrive (exactly, or under a mask), send those bytes after N ms, give or take some jitter", and an optional poll list sends timed steps once or in cycles, which makes the port a master. A seed makes the jitter repeat and a misspelt key is an error that names it. **Tools, Benchmark** (and `vss_cli bench`) sends 1, 10 or 100 MB from one port, counts what arrives on another and reports Passed or Failed and how many bytes were lost.
