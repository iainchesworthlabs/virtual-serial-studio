**Simple view: device settings, COM numbers and network addresses in the forms are applied and checked.** The speed and other settings chosen with **Change** now reach a device that stays open, and Apply says it cannot do that where a build has no way to. A port that chose its COM number is made before one left on Automatic, so Automatic can no longer take the number another row of the same form chose and blame another device. The address of a network device accepts a name or address, `host:port`, an IPv6 address with or without brackets, and `tcp://` or `rfc2217://` in front. A port or protocol typed with the address moves to its own box, and an address that cannot be used (a space, a slash, two ports) stops Create and says how an address is written.
