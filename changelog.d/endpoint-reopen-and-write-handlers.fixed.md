**A write that is thrown away now reports an error, and a reopen no longer leaves a port with two reads.** A write that was waiting when its port was reopened, closed or removed, or when an earlier write failed, was dropped without any answer, so `vss_cli bench` stopped with an exception. Every waiting write now ends with an error. Changing a port's settings while data was arriving could leave the port with two reads at once, which could lose or repeat bytes, and a write made during a reopen could be sent twice.
