**A shared bus can be a half-duplex line, like a two-wire RS-485 pair.** Tick **Half-duplex line** on the Shared bus page of **Connect ports** (Advanced) or in the bus's settings, or add `"half_duplex": { "bit_rate": 9600 }` to a bus link in a topology file. A message is then on the line for its bytes at that rate, and the other ports hear it when its last byte has left the line. A message that starts while another is on the line, or inside the **turn-around** time after it, is counted and shown in the Monitor and the Log. The messages that collided arrive as sent or, if you choose **Report it and garble the bytes**, with every byte inverted, so a checksum fails as on a real line. **Send only while RTS is high** works for loopback ports only in this build; every other port always sends. A station's own echo is not modelled.
