**Plugins can replay a recording, so Diagnostics and replay and Protocol decoding and triggers no longer say that Studio has no timeline.** A plugin that holds a recording in its data folder can ask Studio to play the bytes the recorded port received onto one of your ports, and the control lines the device drove. Each chunk is written at its recorded time or scaled by `time_scale`, never early. A recording that is missing, damaged or does not fit the port is refused with the reason. Faults are not replayed. Bytes the recorded port sent are played only when the plugin asks with `include_sent`.
