**Plugins can replay a recording, so Diagnostics and replay and Protocol decoding and triggers no longer say that Studio has no timeline.** Studio now offers the `timeline` capability to plugins. A plugin that holds a recording in its data folder can ask Studio to play the bytes the recorded port received onto one of your ports, and the control lines the device drove where the port follows them. The chunks keep their boundaries, and each is written at its recorded time or scaled by `time_scale`, never early. A recording of any size plays, because it is read as it goes. A recording that is missing, damaged or has no place that fits the port is refused with the reason. Faults are not replayed. The bytes the recorded port sent are played only when the plugin asks for them with `include_sent`.
