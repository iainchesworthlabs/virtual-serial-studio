**Plugins can replay a recording, so Diagnostics and replay and Protocol decoding and triggers no longer say that Studio has no timeline.** Studio now offers the `timeline` capability to plugins. A plugin that holds a recording in its data folder can ask Studio to play the bytes the recorded port received onto one of your ports, at their recorded times or scaled by `time_scale`, and a chunk is never written before its time. A recording that is missing, damaged, too large (more than 64 MiB of traffic) or has no place that fits the port is refused with the reason. Line changes, faults and the bytes the recorded port sent are not replayed yet.
