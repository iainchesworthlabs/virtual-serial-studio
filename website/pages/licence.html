The Community edition and the GPL

The Community edition is free software. In short, and subject to the licence itself:

  • You may use it for any purpose, at home or at work, on as many computers as you like. The GPL does not forbid commercial use.
  • You may read, build and change the source.
  • You may pass on copies, changed or not. If you do, you must pass on the same freedoms: give the recipient the source of your version under the GPL, and keep the notices.
  • There is no warranty. The program is provided as it is, to the extent the law allows.

Programs that only talk to Studio through a serial port, a network endpoint or the plugin protocol are separate programs. Using Studio does not put your own software, your test scripts or your captured data under the GPL.

Read the GNU General Public License, version 3, at gnu.org. The full text is also in the file LICENSE in the repository and in the About dialog of the application.

Software from others

Studio is built with Qt, Boost and other components, each under its own licence. They are listed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md in the repository and on the Licences tab of the About dialog. No fonts are bundled.

The Pro and Enterprise parts

Pro and Enterprise are in development. When they are released, the official plugins, the licence files and the Windows driver binary that is signed through Microsoft will be supplied under commercial terms, which will be published here. The intended outline is:

  • Pro is an annual licence for each seat. A seat is two computers: the one you work at and one other that you control or monitor from it. The serial device can be on either.
  • Enterprise is a site licence with a support agreement, at a price and on a term agreed with the customer.
  • A trial is 14 days with every Pro feature.
  • When a licence ends, the Pro parts stop after a period of grace and Studio continues as the Community edition. Your ports, setups and files stay yours and stay usable.
  • A licence is checked offline. No activation server is involved and no information about you or your use is sent to us.

The commercial terms apply only to the Pro parts. They do not take away any right the GPL gives you in the Community edition.

Names and marks

The GPL covers the code. It does not give permission to use the name Virtual Serial Studio or its icon to present a changed version as the official one. If you publish a changed version, please give it a name of its own.
