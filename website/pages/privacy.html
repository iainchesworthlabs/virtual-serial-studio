Privacy

We collect nothing

DRAFT: not yet reviewed by a solicitor

We have no data about you, because neither the program nor this website gathers any.

The application

  • No telemetry and no analytics. Studio does not report usage, crashes, port names, traffic or anything else.
  • No account. You do not sign in to use it.
  • No network connection of its own. Studio connects to a network only for an endpoint that you create, to the address that you give it. It does not check for updates.
  • Your traffic stays on your computer. Captures, setups and logs are files that you save where you choose.
  • Diagnostic bundles are yours. A bundle is saved as a file. It leaves your computer only if you send it, and the contents of your traffic are left out unless you tick them.
  • Licence files are checked offline. When the Pro edition is released, its licence will be a file that Studio verifies on your computer. There will be no activation server.

This website

  • No cookies and nothing kept in your browser.
  • No scripts. The pages are plain HTML and CSS.
  • No analytics, trackers, advertising or social media buttons.
  • Nothing loaded from other sites. The fonts are your own system's, and every image and style sheet comes from this site.
  • No forms. The site does not ask for your name or your email address.

Where other parties are involved

Three things are outside our control, and you should know about them.

  • Hosting. This site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and the page you ask for when you visit, and keeps logs for its own security purposes. We do not receive those logs. GitHub's privacy statement explains what it does with them.
  • Downloads. The download links go to GitHub Releases, so GitHub serves the files and sees the request.
  • Issues. If you open an issue on GitHub, what you write is public and is held by GitHub under your GitHub account.

If you write to us

If you contact us about a licence or support once those are offered, we will keep your message and your address for as long as is needed to answer you and to keep the records the law requires, and we will not pass them to anyone else for marketing. This paragraph will be completed before sales open.
