How a licence works

A licence is a file. Studio checks it on your own computer with a key that is built into the program. There is no activation server and nothing is sent anywhere, so it works on a machine with no network.

Trial
14 days, with every Pro feature. A trial licence is issued when you ask for one.
Annual
One year. You renew by loading a new file.
Enterprise
A term we agree with you, as a site licence or bound to named machines.
When a licence ends
Studio says “licence expired” and shows how to renew. The plugins carry on for a further 28 days, and then Studio goes back to being the Community edition. It keeps every port, setup and file. Nothing you made is locked away.
The signed driver
The Community driver is test-signed: Windows trusts it only on a machine where our certificate authority has been installed. The Pro driver is signed through Microsoft and installs on an ordinary Windows machine. The source of both is the same and is published.

Questions

Can I use Community at work?
Yes. The GPL allows commercial use. If you change the program and pass it on, the GPL asks you to pass on your source as well. More about the licence.
Can I buy Pro today?
Not yet. The plugins, the licence files and the signed driver are being built. This page describes the plan so that you can tell us whether it suits you.
What will Pro cost?
The price has not been set. It will be published here before Pro goes on sale.
Can I write my own plugin without Pro?
Yes. The plugin protocol is documented and free to use, and Community loads third-party plugins after it has warned you. Pro is the maintained, supported and tested set. How plugins work.
Does anything phone home to count seats?
No. A seat is kept by the licence file and the licence terms. A lab that wants a hard count asks for an Enterprise licence that names machines.
