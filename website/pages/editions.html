How a licence works

A licence is a file. Studio checks it on your own computer with a key that is built into the program. There is no activation server, so it works on a machine with no network.

Trial
14 days, with every Pro feature. A trial licence is issued when you ask for one.
Annual
One year. You renew by loading a new file.
Enterprise
A site licence, or a licence bound to named machines. The price and the term are agreed with you.
When a licence ends
Studio shows that the licence has ended and how to renew. The official plugins carry on for a further 28 days, and then they switch off and Studio goes back to being the Community edition. A trial has no further days. Studio keeps every port, setup and file.
The signed driver
The Community driver is test-signed: Windows trusts it only on a machine where our certificate authority has been installed. The Pro driver is signed through Microsoft and installs on an ordinary Windows machine. The source of both is the same and is published.

Questions

Can I use Community at work?
Yes. The GPL allows commercial use. If you change the program and pass it on, the GPL asks you to pass on your source as well. Licence.
Can I buy Pro today?
Not yet. The plugins, the licence files and the signed driver are being built. This page describes the plan.
What will Pro cost?
The price has not been set. It will be published here before Pro goes on sale.
Can I write my own plugin without Pro?
Yes. The plugin API is documented and free to use, and Community loads third-party plugins after it has warned you. The official plugins that we build on it, such as remote control, are the Pro edition. Plugins.
Does Studio contact a server to count seats?
No. A seat is kept by the licence file and the licence terms. A lab that wants to limit use to named computers asks for an Enterprise licence that names them.
