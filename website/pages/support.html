Writing a report

  1. Save a diagnostic bundle. In Studio, choose File, then Diagnostic bundle. It saves a ZIP with your setup, the event log, the environment and the port counters. The contents of your traffic are left out unless you tick them, and nothing is sent unless you send it.
  2. Say what you expected and what happened. Include the smallest setup that shows the fault.
  3. Leave out other people's data. Do not attach traffic captured from equipment that is not yours.
